📖 Key terms

Quadratic function

A function whose rule has a squared term: y=ax^2+bx+c with a\ne0. Its graph is a parabola.

Vertex & axis of symmetry

The vertex is the highest or lowest point. The axis x=-\tfrac{b}{2a} splits the parabola into mirror halves.

Zeros (x-intercepts)

Inputs where the output is 0. In factored form a(x-p)(x-q) they are p and q.

Standard form

y=ax^2+bx+c. The y-intercept is c. If a>0 it opens up; if a<0 it opens down.

Factored form

y=a(x-p)(x-q). Shows the zeros. The vertex's x is the midpoint \tfrac{p+q}{2}.

Vertex form

y=a(x-h)^2+k. Shows the vertex (h,k). a stretches, compresses or flips the parent y=x^2.

🎢 Parabola lab: three forms, one graph

Pick a form and move the sliders. The lab converts to the other two forms, marks the vertex, the zeros and the y-intercept, and draws the graph on a square grid. Try the textbook parabolas with the presets.

🏁 Growth race: quadratic vs exponential

Compare c\cdot x^2 with b^x. A quadratic can lead for a while, but an exponential always wins in the end. Try 10x² vs 2ˣ (2ˣ pulls ahead at x = 10) and 100x² vs 4ˣ (at x = 6).

🎆 Projectile launcher

Height in feet after t seconds: h(t)=-16t^2+v_0t+h_0, where v_0 is the starting upward speed and h_0 the starting height. Try the cannon: v_0=406, h_0=10.

💰 Revenue maximizer

Each time the price rises, fewer people buy. With demand m-kx and price x, revenue is R(x)=x(m-kx). The best price is the vertex. Try the theater: (400-8p)p peaks at 25 with 5000.
