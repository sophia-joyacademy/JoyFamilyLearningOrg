Multiplying and Dividing Fractions and Mixed Numbers

Multiply fractions by whole numbers, fractions and mixed numbers, simplify before you multiply, divide a fraction by a whole number, and divide a whole number by a unit fraction.

📖 Key terms

"Of" means ×

34 of 16 = 34 × 16 = 12.

Simplify first

Divide a numerator and a denominator by a common factor before multiplying. The numbers stay small.

Product

The answer to a multiplication. Multiplying by a fraction less than 1 makes the product smaller than the other factor.

Unit fraction

A fraction with numerator 1, like 13. Dividing by a unit fraction multiplies by its denominator: 4 ÷ 13 = 4 × 3.

Reciprocal

Flip a fraction: the reciprocal of 18 is 8. Dividing by a whole number is the same as multiplying by its reciprocal.

Improper fraction

Turn a mixed number into one before multiplying: 359 = 329.

➗ Dividing a fraction by a whole number

Cut each piece into equal smaller pieces. Dividing by n is the same as multiplying by 1n.

🔢 Dividing a whole number by a unit fraction

How many pieces of size 1d fit into n wholes? Each whole holds d of them.

🚧 Common traps

Multiplying doesn't always make it bigger

Multiplying by a fraction less than 1 gives a smaller number: 12 × 12 = 6. Multiplying by an improper fraction gives a bigger one.

Don't cross-simplify across a + sign

You can only cancel in multiplication. Never cancel in an addition or subtraction.

Dividing a fraction by a whole number

17 ÷ 8 = 156, not 8 sevenths. The pieces get smaller, so the denominator grows.

Whole number ÷ unit fraction

4 ÷ 13 = 4 × 3 = 12, not 43. There are 12 thirds in 4 wholes.

Keep the whole number

12 × 359: don't multiply only the fraction part. Change to 329 first.

"Of the remainder"

In a two-step problem, the second fraction is of what is left, not of the original total.
