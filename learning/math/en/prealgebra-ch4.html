Pre-Algebra · Chapter 4

Fractions

Parts of a whole. See fractions, simplify them, then multiply, divide, add and subtract them, including mixed numbers, and solve equations with fractions.

Key terms

Fraction

Written ab. The numerator a counts the parts included. The denominator b is the number of equal parts in the whole.

Proper & improper

Proper: a < b, less than 1. Improper: a ≥ b, at least 1.

Mixed number

A whole number plus a proper fraction, such as 213.

Equivalent fractions

Fractions with the same value, such as 12 = 24.

Simplified fraction

No common factors in the numerator and denominator.

Reciprocal

The reciprocal of ab is ba. A number times its reciprocal is 1.

LCD

The least common denominator of two fractions is the LCM of their denominators.

Complex fraction

A fraction whose numerator or denominator contains a fraction.

✂️ Simplify a fraction

Factor the top and bottom into primes, cross out the common factors, and multiply what is left.

    Equivalent Fractions Property

    a/b = (a·c)/(b·c). Removing a common factor c does not change the value.

    When there are no common factors left, the fraction is simplified. The product of the crossed-out factors is the GCF.

    🔁 Improper fractions ↔ mixed numbers

    Pick a direction, enter the numbers, and step through.

      Improper → mixed
      1. Divide the numerator by the denominator.
      2. Quotient = whole number, remainder = new numerator, divisor = denominator.
      Mixed → improper
      1. Multiply the whole number by the denominator.
      2. Add the numerator.
      3. Write the sum over the original denominator.

      ✖️ Multiply & divide fractions

      Multiply across. To divide, flip the second fraction (use its reciprocal) and multiply. Then simplify.

      ×
        Rules
        • (a/b)·(c/d) = ac/bd. Simplify before you multiply: the numbers stay small.
        • (a/b) ÷ (c/d) = (a/b)·(d/c)
        • Same signs → positive. Different signs → negative.
        • You can never divide by 0.

        ➕ Add & subtract fractions

        Same denominator? Combine the tops. Different denominators? Find the LCD, rewrite both fractions, then combine.

        +
          Find the LCD
          1. Factor each denominator into primes.
          2. Match primes in columns, bring down each column.
          3. Multiply. That is the LCM of the denominators, which is the LCD.

          🧮 Add & subtract mixed numbers

          Write each mixed number as an improper fraction, combine them with a common denominator, then convert back.

          +
            Strategy
            1. Convert to improper fractions.
            2. Find the LCD and rewrite.
            3. Add or subtract the numerators.
            4. Write the answer as a mixed number and simplify.

            Subtracting a larger number from a smaller one gives a negative answer.

            ⚖️ Solve equations with fractions

            Pick a type, enter the fractions, and step through. Do the same thing to both sides. You can also test your own guess.

              Is it a solution?

              Substitute your number and see if the equation is true.
              Addition / Subtraction Property

              If a = b, then a + c = b + c and a − c = b − c.

              Multiplication Property

              If a = b, then ac = bc. Multiply both sides by the same number.

              Strategies cheat sheet

              Simplify a fraction
              1. Factor the top and bottom into primes.
              2. Remove the common factors.
              3. Multiply what is left.
              Multiply or divide
              1. Convert mixed numbers to improper fractions.
              2. Multiply across (flip the second fraction first when dividing).
              3. Simplify.
              Add or subtract
              1. Find the LCD.
              2. Convert each fraction to an equivalent fraction with the LCD.
              3. Add or subtract the numerators.
              4. Simplify.
              Subtract mixed numbers
              1. Compare the fractions. If the top one is smaller, borrow 1 whole from the top whole number.
              2. Subtract the fractions, then the whole numbers.
              3. Or: use improper fractions and convert back.
              Complex fractions
              1. Simplify the top and the bottom.
              2. Rewrite as a division problem.
              3. Multiply by the reciprocal and simplify.
              Solve a fraction equation
              1. Undo + with −, − with +.
              2. For (a/b)x, multiply both sides by the reciprocal b/a.
              3. Simplify and check.
