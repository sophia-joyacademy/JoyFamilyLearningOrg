Pre-Algebra · Chapter 6

Percents

A percent is a ratio out of 100. Convert between percents, fractions and decimals; solve percent problems; find percent increase and decrease; work out sales tax, discounts, commission, mark-up and simple interest; and solve proportions.

Key terms

Percent

A ratio whose denominator is 100. 35% means 35 out of 100.

Percent increase

The percent the amount of increase is of the original amount.

Percent decrease

The percent the amount of decrease is of the original amount.

Sales tax

A percent of the purchase price.

Commission

A percentage of total sales, determined by the rate of commission.

Discount

A percent off the original price, determined by the discount rate.

Mark-up

The amount added to the wholesale price, determined by the mark-up rate.

Simple interest

Interest earned on a principal P at annual rate r for t years: I = Prt.

Proportion

An equation saying two ratios or rates are equal: a/b = c/d. Read “a is to b as c is to d”.

    🧮 Percent equations

    Every percent problem has three parts: the amount, the percent and the base. Know two, find the third. The key words: “of” means multiply, “is” means equals.

        🛍️ Sales tax, discount, commission and mark-up

        All four use the same move: rate × amount. Then add or subtract to finish the story.

        %

          • sales tax = tax rate · purchase price

            sale price = original − discount
          • commission = rate of commission · total sales
          • mark-up = mark-up rate · wholesale price
            list price = wholesale + mark-up

          Money answers are rounded to the nearest cent.

              Strategies cheat sheet

              Percent → fraction / decimal
              1. Write the percent over 100.
              2. Simplify the fraction.
              3. Or divide by 100 (move the point 2 places left) for the decimal.
              Decimal / fraction → percent
              1. Fraction: divide the top by the bottom first.
              2. Move the decimal point 2 places right.
              3. Add the % sign.
              Percent equation
              1. Name what you are looking for.
              2. Translate: “of” → ·, “is” → =, “what” → n.
              3. Change the percent to a decimal.
              4. Solve, then answer in a sentence and check.
              Percent change
              1. Amount of change = bigger − smaller.
              2. Divide the change by the original amount.
              3. Move the point 2 places right.
              Tax, discount, commission, mark-up
              1. Amount = rate · base.
              2. Tax and mark-up: add to the base.
              3. Discount: subtract from the base.
              Proportions
              1. Write the proportion with x for the unknown.
              2. Cross multiply: a·d = b·c.
              3. Divide both sides by the number next to x.
              4. Check that the cross products match.
