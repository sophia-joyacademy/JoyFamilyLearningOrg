Math Models and Geometry

Use the 7-step problem-solving strategy on money (coin) problems and on geometry: angles, triangles, the Pythagorean theorem, rectangles, trapezoids, circles, volume and surface area. Then solve a formula for any variable.

Key terms

Complementary angles

Two angles whose measures add to 90°.

Supplementary angles

Two angles whose measures add to 180°.

Right triangle

A triangle with one 90° angle. The side opposite it is the hypotenuse; the other two are the legs.

Isosceles / equilateral

Isosceles: two equal sides. Equilateral: all three sides equal.

Perimeter

The distance around a figure.

Area

The measure of the surface a figure covers.

Trapezoid

A four-sided figure with two parallel sides (the bases) and two sides that are not.

Similar figures

Same shape, different size: equal angles and sides in the same ratio.

Irregular figure

Not a standard shape. Split it into standard shapes and add the areas.

                Strategies cheat sheet

                Coin problems
                1. Make a table: type, number, value, total value.
                2. Let x = the number of one type; the other is (total − x).
                3. Add the total values to equal the total money.
                4. Solve, then check the count and the money.
                Geometry problems
                1. Draw the figure and label what you know.
                2. Pick the formula and substitute.
                3. Solve for the unknown.
                4. Check, and answer with units (square units for area, cubic for volume).
                Which formula?
                • Distance around: perimeter or circumference.
                • Space covered: area.
                • Space inside a solid: volume.
                • Outside of a solid: surface area.
                Solving a formula
                • Treat the other letters like numbers.
                • Undo what is done to the variable, in reverse order.
                • Do the same thing to both sides.
