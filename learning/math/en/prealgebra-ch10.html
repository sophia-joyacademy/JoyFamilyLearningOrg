Pre-Algebra · Chapter 10

Polynomials

Name polynomials by terms and degree, add, subtract and multiply them, use the exponent rules, write numbers in scientific notation, and factor out the greatest common factor.

Key terms

Monomial

A term of the form axm: a constant times a variable to a whole-number power.

Polynomial

A monomial, or monomials combined by addition or subtraction.

Binomial · trinomial

A polynomial with exactly two terms · exactly three terms.

Degree

Degree of a term = the exponent of its variable (a constant has degree 0). Degree of a polynomial = the highest degree of its terms.

Zero and negative exponents

a0 = 1 (a ≠ 0) and a−n = 1/an.

Scientific notation

a × 10n with 1 ≤ a < 10 and n an integer.

Greatest common factor (GCF)

The largest expression that is a factor of all the terms.

Exponent properties

PropertyRule
Productam · an = am+n (add the exponents)
Power(am)n = am·n (multiply the exponents)
Product to a power(ab)m = ambm
Quotientam ÷ an = am−n (subtract the exponents)
Quotient to a power(a/b)m = am/bm
Zero exponenta0 = 1 (a ≠ 0)
Negative exponenta−n = 1/an

➕ Add and subtract polynomials

Only like terms (same variable and same power) can be combined. Edit the coefficients. When subtracting, every sign in the second polynomial flips.

      ✖️ Multiply polynomials

      Multiply every term of one polynomial by every term of the other. The area model shows each partial product.

        Area model

        Each cell is one term × one term. Add all the cells, then combine like terms.

        🔭 Scientific notation

        Very big and very small numbers are written as a × 10n. Move the decimal point so that a is at least 1 and less than 10.

          🧩 Factor out the GCF

          Find what every term shares, then use the distributive property in reverse. Leave a coefficient at 0 to use just two terms.

            Strategies cheat sheet

            Add / subtract
            1. Write each polynomial in parentheses.
            2. For subtraction, flip every sign in the second one.
            3. Group like terms (same variable, same power).
            4. Add the coefficients; the exponents stay.
            Multiply
            1. Multiply each term of the first by each term of the second.
            2. Binomials: First, Outer, Inner, Last.
            3. Multiply coefficients, add exponents of x.
            4. Combine like terms.
            Exponents
            • Same base, multiplying: add exponents.
            • Same base, dividing: subtract exponents.
            • Power of a power: multiply exponents.
            • a⁰ = 1, and a−n is 1/an.
            Factoring the GCF
            1. Find the GCF of the coefficients and the lowest power of x.
            2. Divide each term by the GCF.
            3. Write GCF( … ).
            4. Check by multiplying back.
