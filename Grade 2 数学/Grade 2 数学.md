**Grade 2 数学**  
  
1. **Number Sense**  
1.1 Students understand the relationship between numbers,  
quantities, and place value in whole numbers up to 1,000.  
Strategy 1: Write seven hundred fifty-three in expanded form.  
There is a 7 in the hundreds place.  
There is a 5 in the tens place.  
There is a 3 in the ones place.  
How can you write this number?  
Strategy 2: Use place value to form a whole number.  
The table below shows the correct place values of the digits.  
  
1.2 Students estimate, calculate, and solve problems involving  
addition and subtraction of two- and three-digit numbers.  
  
Strategy 1: Add to check a subtraction problem.  
Add the difference (13) to the number subtracted (24). The sum should equal 37.  
  
Strategy 2: Use regrouping to solve an addition problem.  
Add the digits in the ones column. 9 + 3 = 12  
The sum is 10 or more, so we will regroup. Regroup  
12 as 1 ten and 2 ones.  
Write the ones. Then add the tens and hundreds.  
  
1.3 Students model and solve simple problems involving  
multiplication and division.  
  
Strategy 1: Use repeated addition to model multiplication. How many  
rows of 4 are there for the product 3 × 4?  
Multiplication is repeated addition.  
  
Strategy 2: Model a division problem.  
We can make a model to solve. Divide 16 students into 4 equal groups.  
  
1.4 Students understand that fractions and decimals may refer to parts of a set and parts of a whole.  
  
Strategy 1: Count the number of shaded parts. Compare this to the number of total parts.  
The circle is divided into equal parts. How many equal parts do you see? How many of the equal parts are shaded? Use this information to write the fraction.  
  
Strategy 2: Count the number of shaded circles. Compare this to the total number of circles.  
How many circles are there in all? How many of them are shaded?  
  
1.5 Students model and solve problems by representing, adding, and subtracting amounts of money.  
  
Strategy 1: Add values of coins to find the total amount of money.  
Strategy 2: Use the decimal point and dollar sign to show amounts of money.  
Write the dollars in between the dollar sign and the decimal point.  
Write the cents on the right side of the decimal point.  
  
1.6 Students use estimation strategies in computation and problem solving that involve numbers that use the ones, tens, hundreds, and thousands places.  
  
Strategy 1: Choose the best unit for the estimate. Then think how many of those units make up the object.  
Is a foot the unit you would use to measure the length of your math book? You can get rid of answers that do not make sense.  
  
Strategy 2: Use what you know about time to make a reasonable estimate.  
Estimate the amount of sleep you get in one night. Then think about the number of nights in one week. Use repeated addition to solve.  
  
2. Algebra and Functions  
  
Students model, represent, and interpret number relationships to create and solve problems involving addition and subtraction.  
  
Strategy 1: Choose the number that will give the same sum on both sides of the equals sign.  
Remember that 2 + 5 = 5 + 2. It does not matter what order you add whole numbers.  
  
Strategy 2: Let 【】 	represent the number of stuffed animals Carrie won.  
Carrie had eleven stuffed animals. She added 【】 stuffed animals to this number. How can you write this sum?  
  
3. Measurement and Geometry  
  
3.1 Students understand that measurement is accomplished by identifying a unit of measure, iterating (repeating) that unit, and comparing it to the item to be measured.  
  
Strategy: Add the length of time at practice to the starting time. This will give you end ending time.  
Start at 4:00. Add two hours. The answer is the time that practice ended.  
  
Strategy: Use an inch ruler to find the length of the ant.  
Place one end of the ruler at the end of one ant.  
Count the number of inches to the end of the other end.  
  
3.2 Students identify and describe the attributes of common figures in the plane and of common objects in space.  
  
Reading Hint: An edge is a line segment where two faces meet.  
Count the edges of the prism. Remember to count the ones that you cannot see.  
  
Reading Hint: A triangle is a shape that has three sides.  
Think about triangles. We know that triangles have 3 sides.  
Look for the answer choice where both shapes have 3 sides.  
  
4. Statistics, Data Analysis and Probability  
  
4.1 Students collect numerical data and record, organize, display, and interpret the data on bar graphs and other representations.  
  
Strategy: Use a table to find the range of heights.  
Who is the tallest student? Who is the shortest?  
Subtract to find the difference.  
  
Strategy: Use a table to find the model.  
Which number do you see most often?  
  
4.2 Students demonstrate an understanding of patterns and how patterns grow and describe them in general ways.  
  
Strategy: Look for a pattern in the table. Describe how the number of tires can be found.  
Two tires are needed for each bicycle.  
Multiply the number of bicycles by 2 to find the number of tires needed.  
  
Strategy 2: Look for the patten in the set of numbers.   
The pattern is found by adding two each time. You can find the next number by adding 2.   
  
5. Mathematical Reasoning  
  
5.1 Students make decisions about how to set up a problem.  
Strategy: Use a sketch to help you solve the problem.  
Which sketch shows a cone at the beginning, a cone at the end, and a cone every ten feet?  
  
Strategy: Decide which number sentence will help you solve the problem.  
How many graham crackers are in each group?  
How many groups are there?  
  
5.2 Students solve problems and justify their reasoning.  
Strategy: Justify your answer by explaining your work.  
To show twice as many, we can multiply by 2.  
Which equation equals twice as many as the number of tulips?   
  
5.3 Students note connections between one problem and another.  
Strategy: Note differences and similarities between plane shapes and solid shapes.  
List the traits of each shape to determine which shape is different.
